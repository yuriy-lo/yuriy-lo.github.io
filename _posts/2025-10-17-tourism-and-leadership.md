Best book on people management that I ever read. It is in Russian and was never translated.

Book is called "Psychological climate in a group of tourists" meaning a group of people travelling in a wild autonomously. It was written by Edgar Linchevskiy, PhD in medicine, associate professor and practicing psychiatrist but also a sportsman who did many expeditions as a group leader. Although the book describes a very specific sport and leisure popular in the USSR, the main concepts are applicable to many kinds of teams. It is meant to be a handbook of leader and is very practical.

Proper functioning of a team of alpinists during autonomous expedition is a question of life and death, so Linchevskiy focused on core concepts: what should be relationships and mindset in a team to make journey safe and successful, why don't they appear automatically, what can destroy them and what is job and responsibility of leader in shaping and guiding a team that can succeed. A lot of practical advice and examples on conflicts management, development of communication skills, maintaining respectful but solid relationships and proper planning make a book fascinating reading.

Long story short, team is temporary thing and every team member has his own personal goals, priorities and expectations. Dissatisfaction, lack of unity and leadership are frequent reasons why teams break up and fail. Responsibility of leader is to develop common goal and plan that is shared by all team members. Art of leadership is to fit personal goals and wishes into shared goal so everyone is happy to work towards the common goal and implement common plan. Skill of leadership is to recognize and fix destructive dynamics in relationships.

And all that applies to engineering teams. When one is waiting for his retirement, other just started his career and third wishes new opportunities and higher positions, it may sound challenging for leader to deliver a result. But team, that includes experience and knowledge, freshness and openness to everything new, wish to improve and stand out, have to succeed. For team to be productive, everyone should know how team goal and plan help him to achieve his personal goals.
