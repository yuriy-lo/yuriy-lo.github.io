Many years ago, I was impressed that professional athletes in the NHL have clauses in their contracts that prohibit them from participating in extreme or dangerous activities. An athlete's body is a tool that must function properly. Professional behavior means avoiding activities that could damage the body and keeping it in good shape. For a software engineer, the brain is the tool, and it must function properly. Mental hygiene is simply a professional behavior. We deal with complex problems, uncertainty, pressure, and ambiguity. Did anyone have a course at university on how to manage stress and how to recover? I believe it is rare.

There will be no recommendations. Take care of yourself.
