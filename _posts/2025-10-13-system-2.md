GUI design, decision-making research, and LLMs: what common ground do these books share?

They describe a system that:
* Processes incoming information to generate responses.
* Operates quickly and automatically.
* Faces limitations when learning new concepts.
* Struggles with complex logic and calculations.
* Is non-deterministic, yet still produces useful results.
* Cannot explain the reasoning behind its outputs.

Jef Raskin calls it the "unconscious" part of us. Daniel Kahneman calls it "System 1" – the way we usually think. It's mind-blowing how much LLMs resemble that part of our brains! But the "conscious system," or "System 2", which handles logic and math, isn't quite there yet. Let's see if reasoning models can bring us closer.
