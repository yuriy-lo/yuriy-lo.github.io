If you open your hand, your phone will drop and break. That's gravity law. Here's what's interesting: these three observations about how groups work follow similar patterns—predictable and inevitable, like physics rather than psychology.

Conway's law: merge two teams, and developers will attempt to merge their repositories (and vice versa).

Brooks's law: add more people to the project, and release will be delayed even further.

Dunbar's number: once your team exceeds a certain headcount, people will change the way they talk to each other.
